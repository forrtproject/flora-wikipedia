Research material shared for review; not a finished publication.

The cards were prepared with AI and nobody has checked them yet. Read the articles and sources before acting, and write any edit in your own words. The earlier AI-written draft wording is not included.

suggestions.json holds the cards; suggestions.csv has one row per study. about.md describes the method and coverage, and contributor-workflow.md explains how to review a card and record a decision. papers-not-obtained.csv lists the papers we could not get in full, with notes on where we looked. evidence-packets.zip holds the evidence the AI was given for each study. source-cache.zip holds publication details, abstracts, possible review articles, revision checks, the request log and the saved Wikipedia policies; a possible review article is a lead, not a checked source. The other files record the selection, the AI comparisons, the checks and the costs.

Cached full texts stay in runs/edit-prototype-en/cache/fulltext and are not included here, because some free-to-read copies have no reuse licence.
